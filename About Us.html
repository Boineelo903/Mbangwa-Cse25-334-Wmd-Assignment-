About Nolo Cosmetics

Founded in 2020, Nolo Cosmetics was born out of a passion for inclusive, high‑quality cosmetics. We believe makeup is not just about looking good – it's about feeling confident, expressing your personality, and celebrating your unique beauty.

Our Mission

To provide cruelty‑free, ethically sourced makeup that empowers everyone to feel beautiful in their own skin.

Our Vision

To become Africa's most trusted and loved makeup brand, known for innovation, inclusivity, and sustainability.
